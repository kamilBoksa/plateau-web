Questions

Before you ask.

Do I lose my workouts if I don’t subscribe?

No. Your entire history stays readable forever on the free plan, along with unlimited logging and unlimited templates. Pro adds analysis on top of that data; it never locks the data itself.

Are there ads?

None, on either plan. An advert has no business interrupting a working set, and we would rather be paid by the people using the app.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Your workouts are stored on the phone itself, so a basement gym with no reception changes nothing. Exercise animations are cached after the first time you view them.

How do I cancel Pro?

In your App Store or Google Play account settings, not in the app. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the period you have already paid for runs to its end. Refunds go through Apple or Google under their own policies.

Which devices?

iPhone and Android, from a single codebase.

Can I get my data out?

CSV Export is on the Pro roadmap and is not built yet. Until it ships, your log lives in a standard database on your own device.
