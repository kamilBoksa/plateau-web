The core loop

Log a set in two taps.

Start from a template or build the session as you go. Weight and reps carry over from last time, so most sets are one tap to confirm.

  • Templates for the sessions you repeat — reorder them however you train
  • A rest timer that keeps running while you scroll
  • Per-exercise notes that resurface the next time you train it
  • Kilograms or pounds, switched whenever you like
  • Interrupted mid-session? The draft is waiting when you come back

Why it’s called Plateau

See the plateau coming.

A stall is obvious in hindsight and invisible while it happens. Every exercise carries its own numbers so you catch the flattening early.

  • Personal records surfaced the moment you hit them
  • Estimated one-rep max, tracked over time
  • Volume and trend charts per exercise
  • Pick your window — the last month or everything you’ve logged

Your training log

Never lose a workout.

Every session you finish is kept, in full, for as long as you want it. Not ninety days — all of it, on the free plan.

  • A calendar of everything you’ve trained
  • A consistency graph that makes gaps impossible to rationalise
  • Post-workout summaries that call out the records you just set
  • Stored on your phone, so the gym’s dead signal doesn’t matter

Exercise library

Every exercise, demonstrated.

Seventy-two movements across seven body parts — and every single one has an animated demo. Not a catalogue of names you have to look up elsewhere.

  • Animated demonstrations for all 72 exercises
  • Filter by body part or exercise type
  • Step-by-step form notes on each movement
  • Add your own exercises when your gym has something unusual
