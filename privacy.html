Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

Plateau is a strength-training log. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who else can see it. It is short because the app does comparatively little with your data.

The data controller responsible for this processing is Kamil Boksa, ul. Majora Sucharskiego 13, 33-180 Gromnik, Poland (contact details below).

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and an account identifier, handled by Google Firebase Authentication. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive whatever that provider passes on — typically an email address (or Apple’s relay address if you chose to hide yours) and, if you allow it, the name on your account.
  • Your workouts. Exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, notes, templates and workout history.
  • Subscription status. Whether a Pro subscription is active, managed through RevenueCat. We never see your card details; those stay with Apple or Google.
  • Technical data our providers need to run. When the app talks to Firebase or RevenueCat, those services necessarily see your IP address, device type and operating system version, and generate an app-instance identifier. We do not use any of this to build a profile of you, and we do not combine it with your workout data. It is a by-product of the app connecting to a server at all.

Legal basis for processing

  • Account details and your workouts. Necessary to perform our contract with you — providing the app you signed up to use.
  • Subscription status. Necessary to perform our contract with you — providing the Pro features you paid for.
  • Security and app-health information collected by our processors (for example, Firebase). Our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working.

Where your workouts live

By default, your training data is stored in a database on your own device and is not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes that data.

Cloud Backup & Sync is a Pro feature that turns on automatically once your subscription is active — there is no separate switch to flip. While it is on, your exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, notes, templates and workout history are copied to a private, per-account store in Google Firestore, scoped so only your signed-in account can read or write it. If you cancel or let your Pro subscription lapse, syncing stops but data already uploaded is not automatically deleted from Firestore; delete your account (see “Your rights” below) to remove it immediately. We don’t currently offer a way to remove only your cloud backup while keeping your account and local data — email us and we’ll delete it manually.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDK is present in the app.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data. There is nobody to sell it to who we would want to do business with.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Google Firebase — authentication, delivery of the exercise animation files, and (for Pro subscribers with Cloud Backup & Sync active) storage of your synced workout data in Firestore. Requests reach Google’s servers.
  • RevenueCat — subscription entitlement state.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing and subscription management.

International data transfers

Firebase and RevenueCat process data on servers outside the EEA, including in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is protected by appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Your account details and any synced workout data are kept for as long as your account exists. Delete the account and they go, as described below. Workout data on your device stays there until you delete the app or the account. Our payment providers keep transaction records for as long as their own tax and accounting obligations require, independently of us.

Your rights

You can delete your account from within the app, which removes your authentication record, the workout data held on the device, and — if Cloud Backup & Sync was ever active — the copy of that data held in Firestore. If you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR additionally gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Write to us and we will act on it. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in Poland, the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Children

Plateau is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If the change is material — a new category of data, a new recipient, a new purpose — we will give reasonable notice before it takes effect, by email to the address on your account or by a notice in the app, and where the law requires your consent we will ask for it rather than assume it. Otherwise, the current version is always the one on this page.

Contact

Plateau is developed by Kamil Boksa, ul. Majora Sucharskiego 13, 33-180 Gromnik, Poland. Questions about this policy: support@getplateau.app
